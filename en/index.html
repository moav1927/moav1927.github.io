    What is a PDF data extractor?

    It is a tool that reads your documents and copies the data that matters into a table: the invoice number, the date, the tax ID, the total, the title of a drawing or the author of a thesis. It is what you would do by hand, document by document, but in seconds and without typing mistakes.

    A PDF is made to look good, not to be added up or filtered. When you have 10, 50 or 200 of them, copying them into Excel by hand takes hours. The extractor reads them all, classifies them, puts one row per document and calculates the totals.

    Your files are not uploaded

    Everything is read inside your browser. Nothing is sent to a server, there is no account and nothing is kept when you close the page.

    What computer do I need?PDFs with text, Word, Excel and XML are fast on almost any device. Photos and scans are slower: 8 GB of RAM is ideal; 4 GB also works, with patience.See details
    • PDFs with text, Word, Excel and XML: seconds on almost any device, even with 100 files or more.
    • Photos and scanned PDFs (OCR): the heavy part. With 8 GB of RAM and 4 cores, about 3 to 10 seconds per page; with 4 GB, 30 seconds to 1 minute.
    • Minimum: 4 GB of RAM and an up-to-date Chrome, Edge or Firefox. It works on phones, but slower.
    • Tip: close other tabs and upload scans in groups of 5 to 10.
    How does it read documents?With rules, no artificial intelligence: it looks for labels like “Total”, “Invoice No.” or “Scale”. Whatever it cannot find is shown in yellow and in the “To review” column.See details

    In the preview you can fix any value with a double-click: totals are recalculated and the change goes into the Excel file. It recognizes common formats in several languages (“Total:”, “Invoice No.”, “Rechnungsdatum”, “Scale”, “Drawn by”…). Photos and scans are read with OCR (text recognition in images): the first time, about 6 MB are downloaded and kept in the browser.
